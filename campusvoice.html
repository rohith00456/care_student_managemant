    One report, eight checkpoints, zero disappearing acts

    Every submission moves through the same accountable sequence — nothing gets marked done without evidence and a student sign-off.

    01

    Student submits

    Category, department, location, urgency, evidence, and a privacy level are set at the start — public, anonymous, or confidential to a grievance officer.
    02

    System checks it

    Automated checks catch spam, duplicates, and missing detail; a human reviews anything sensitive before it moves forward.
    03

    College verifies & assigns

    A staff member confirms the issue is genuine, assigns an owner, sets a target date, and issues a tracking number.
    04

    Department investigates

    Internal notes, follow-up questions, and a public timeline update — sensitive cases move to a restricted workflow.
    05

    College responds

    What will be done, what won't, and why — a bare "noted" is never treated as a resolution.
    06

    Action is taken

    The owner uploads evidence of progress or completion; dates and changes are recorded so history can't be rewritten.
    07

    Students verify

    Affected students mark the outcome solved, partially solved, or not solved, with a short note.
    08

    Closed — or reopened

    Confirmed fixes close and roll into performance reports. Rejected fixes reopen automatically and escalate.

    Built so criticism doesn't cost you anything

    Protections for students and departments, on both sides of every case.

    Anonymous, not secret

    Choose public, anonymous-to-peers, or confidential-to-an-officer — the form is upfront about what each level actually protects.

    Explainable priority

    Priority scores show their inputs — affected students, urgency, safety risk, time unresolved — never an unexplained number.

    Reasons for "no"

    A rejected or postponed request comes with a stated reason: budget, policy, safety, duplication, or planned future work.

    Verification quorum

    Several affected students can reject a marked-complete fix and send it straight back to the department.

    Duplicate detection

    Typing a complaint surfaces similar open issues first, so support consolidates instead of splintering.

    Recognition, not just complaints

    Students can credit a helpful teacher, a fixed facility, or a well-run event — feedback isn't only ever critical.
